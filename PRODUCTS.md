# AI Builder Tools

All 3 products in this package.

Every entry below is taken from the package's product inventory. Nothing here is a plan or a
roadmap item; all of it is built.

---

## Meta-Brain

release gate + QA suite, green

Describe an intelligent system in plain language and it designs the whole thing: the agents, the memory, the knowledge base, the tools, how the parts communicate and how it reasons. Includes an eight-reviewer architecture review.

## Bridgesmith

6 tests, green

Point it at any website and it generates a complete, runnable server that lets AI agents operate that site, reading the real forms, links and tables on the page and inferring what tools the agent needs.

## Ledgerless

full offline demonstration loop, green

An offline brain for a small business, plus a client-acquisition engine that researches the local market, verifies contact addresses, and drafts outreach grounded in the business's own facts. A message cannot be sent unless it passes the compliance gate.
